# Restaurant Expenses Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add simple, auditable restaurant expenses that reconcile cash drawers, appear in business-day reporting, print on 80mm paper, and never rewrite sales or tax totals.

**Architecture:** Store categories and expense events in two narrow InnoDB tables. Cash-drawer writes lock the open shift, validate available expected cash, and save/cancel atomically; shared expense rollups feed every existing shift calculation so POS, admin, X/Z, and audit output agree. Admin reporting gets one Expenses tab, while permitted cashiers get one touch-friendly POS entry form.

**Tech Stack:** Node.js/Express, MySQL/MariaDB InnoDB, Vue 3, Vitest, Supertest, existing durable print queue and 80mm spooler.

## Global Constraints

- Do not subtract expenses from `sales_collected`, `sales_processed`, `net_revenue_pre_tax`, tax, invoices, or orders.
- `remaining_after_expenses = sales_collected - active recorded expenses` and must not be labeled profit.
- Drawer expenses require `pos.expenses`, the actor's own open shift, an active category, and amount no greater than live expected cash.
- Admin/programmer may record drawer expenses against any open shift or outside-POS expenses; only admin/programmer may manage categories or cancel expenses.
- Entry and cancellation are limited to the current business day; closed shifts and historical periods are immutable.
- Amounts are final paid amounts with two-decimal validation; no supplier accounting, input-tax accounting, attachments, cash-ins, or inventory behavior.
- Save/cancel succeeds independently of printer availability; durable printing may retry and reports allow reprint.
- All new UI copy has natural English and Arabic translations; numeric formatting remains Latin digits and existing JD currency presentation.
